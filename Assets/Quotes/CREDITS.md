# Sentence data credits

This folder holds `quotes.json` (below) and the expression files in `data` (further down). `quotes.json` holds the English sentences with Vietnamese translations that PomoQuote shows: 357 written or collected for PomoQuote (the first four groups below) and 13677 pairs from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) (the last group). It is UTF-8 (no byte order mark). The data is organised in groups; each group has one **category** (`motivation`, `idiom`, `conversation`), one **source** and one **license**, and a sentence can add its own **author**. A group whose licence asks for attribution (Tatoeba's CC BY) must give, on every entry, the id and the author of the English sentence and the id and the author of its Vietnamese translation: the loader refuses the file otherwise. The app and its tests (`PomoQuote.Tests/QuoteDataTests.cs`) reject an entry with an empty field, an unknown category, a duplicate or a missing translation.

**Vietnamese translations of the original 357 sentences** were written for this project (not copied from any source and not word-for-word machine output): where Vietnamese has an equivalent proverb it is used (for example "Hoạn nạn mới biết bạn hiền"), otherwise the sentence is translated for meaning in everyday Vietnamese. They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They have **not been reviewed by a native speaker**; corrections are welcome (edit `quotes.json`, run `dotnet test`).

## Tatoeba sentences (CC BY 2.0 FR)

The last group of `quotes.json` holds 13677 English sentences with their Vietnamese translations from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org), a collaborative database of sentences and translations. Tatoeba's [terms of use](https://tatoeba.org/en/terms_of_use) (section 6.2) put its text sentences under the [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France licence (CC BY 2.0 FR)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/): reuse, modification and distribution are allowed **on condition that the author's name is cited**. The sentences were taken from Tatoeba's public exports of 2026-11-21 (https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/).

**How the obligation is met.**

- Every Tatoeba entry in `quotes.json` records the Tatoeba id and the author (the contributor's user name) of the English sentence (`id`, `author`) and the id and the author of the Vietnamese translation (`vid`, `vauthor`); the sentence's page is `https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/<id>`. The group records the licence and the link to the source.
- The app shows these names: hover the sentence on the widget or in the normal window to see "Tatoeba (tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR. English: sentence #id by author. Vietnamese: sentence #id by author." (`Quote.Attribution`). **Help > About** names Tatoeba and the licence, and `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` lists it.
- **Changes made** (CC BY asks to say so): typographic quotes, apostrophes and dashes were replaced by plain ASCII ones, runs of spaces were collapsed and the Vietnamese text was normalised to Unicode form C. No wording was changed. Only whole pairs were chosen; nothing was merged or rewritten.
- Tatoeba has no warranty on its sentences and is not affiliated with PomoQuote; Tatoeba's name is used only to say where the data comes from.

**How the sentences were chosen** (`tools/import-tatoeba.mjs`, a development tool run by hand; the app never reads the network). `Assets/Quotes/tatoeba-import-report.md` lists the number of pairs left after each step. Summary: 23,285 English-Vietnamese links in the export; both sentences must exist and have a known author (a sentence without an author cannot be credited); typography is cleaned and English must be plain ASCII; length and shape limits so that every sentence fits the widget (3 to 20 words, at most 100 English and 130 Vietnamese characters, a capital letter and a final . ? or !); sentences tagged on Tatoeba as vulgar, slang, ambiguous, non-literal or needing a change are dropped; adult, offensive, violent, drug, alcohol, illness, political, contested-place and religious content is filtered with word lists (deliberately wide, so some harmless sentences are lost); a translation must pass heuristic checks (length and word-count ratios, the same kind of sentence ending, the same numbers, the names kept, tone marks present, no copied English words, no word that occurs almost nowhere else in Tatoeba, which catches typos); one pair is kept for each sentence, preferring translations that Tatoeba users marked "OK"; duplicates of each other and of the 357 existing sentences are removed. 13677 pairs remain. All of them are in the category `conversation` (no new category).

**Limits of this data.** The translations were written by Tatoeba contributors and are not reviewed by PomoQuote's maintainers; the filters are heuristics, not a proof of faithfulness (a read of three random samples, of 120 and 200 pairs while the rules were being tuned and of 120 pairs with the final rules, found about 3 in 100 with a loose or slightly wrong translation or a Vietnamese typo). Tatoeba marks only a small part of its translations as checked ("OK") and does not publish who is a native speaker. Corrections belong on Tatoeba (the data is re-imported from there) or can be made in `quotes.json` by removing the entry.

## Expressions (`data/*.jsonl`)

Besides `quotes.json`, the folder `data` holds JSON Lines files with one expression per line, **one file per source and licence**; no entry mixes two licences. Each entry has an id, a type (idiom, proverb, quote, maxim, expression, phrasal_verb), the English text and meaning, a Vietnamese translation with its **status** (`source`: written in the source; `machine_unreviewed`; `sampled_ok`; `human_reviewed`), who made it, the licence, and the source page. The loader (`Quotes/ExpressionCatalog.cs`) refuses an incomplete or wrongly licensed entry. Licences: [data/LICENSE.md](data/LICENSE.md).

**English Wiktionary** (`wiktionary-*.jsonl`, CC BY-SA 4.0): 3242 entries (1441 idioms, 778 phrasal verbs, 775 common expressions, 248 proverbs). `tools/wiktionary-candidates.mjs` picks them (current general meanings, nothing offensive, vulgar or adult, the most common first), a language model translates them and rates how useful each is (only common and fairly common ones are used), and `tools/wiktionary-build.mjs` runs the checks and writes the files. A second, independent model run translates each Vietnamese line back without seeing the English and compares: only what it calls ok ships. What it calls doubtful was left out and not replaced (owner decision of 2026-11-26); those ids are listed in `tools/wiktionary-dropped.json`, which the build never ships. The counts of every step are in [wiktionary-import-report.md](wiktionary-import-report.md); [review/wiktionary-review-sample.csv](review/wiktionary-review-sample.csv) is a sample for a person to check.

**Limits.** The machine translations have **not been reviewed by a person**; the app says so under each of them. The second pass is also a machine (the same model family), so it can miss errors both make. The ratings and the content filter are judgements, not proof. Corrections: edit the entry (set its status to `human_reviewed`) and run `dotnet test`.

**What was left out on purpose:** no sentence was taken from quote websites, and no modern copyrighted quotation is included.
The data is read through `IQuoteProvider`; only the bundled offline provider exists, and the app makes no network request for sentences.
